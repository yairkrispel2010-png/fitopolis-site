Join the closed test

FITOPOLIS is in a closed test on Android, for people who were invited. Joining takes a few minutes, in two steps.

Before you start

  • An Android phone with a Google account signed in to Google Play. You use the same account in both steps.
  • For now the closed test is on Android only.

Step 1 — join the group

Open the group link and tap join. The group is what lets your account see the app in Google Play.

Go to the test group

Step 2 — join the test and download

After you join the group it can take a few minutes until the next link opens. If it does not work yet, wait and try again.

On your phone, open the test link, tap "Become a tester", then "Download it on Google Play". The app installs like any other.

Open the test in Google Play

After installing

  • Trainers. Open the app, choose "I'm here to coach" and sign in with your phone number.
  • Trainees. Your trainer sends you an invitation, as a link or a code. Open the app, choose "I'm here to train" and enter the code. You can also enter an invitation code here.

Something does not work?

Write to us on the support page, by email or from inside the app, and we will get back to you.
