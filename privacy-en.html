FITOPOLIS Privacy Policy

Pilot version. Effective from 27 October 2026.

This policy uses "he" for readability, and it applies to everyone who uses the app, trainers and trainees.

1. In short

  • FITOPOLIS is an app for personal trainers and the people they coach. The trainer runs the coaching in it, and the trainee logs what he eats, how he trains and what he weighs. They also set meetings and talk in a chat inside it.
  • We keep only what the app needs to work: your phone number so you can sign in, and what you and your trainer record and send each other.
  • Some of it is information about your health: weight, body measurements, what you ate and how you trained. We treat it as sensitive information.
  • Your trainer sees what you record and send him. You do not see the private notes he writes about you, or his payment tracking.
  • What you write for yourself stays on your phone: a note on a weigh-in, a note to yourself on a workout, and the reason you wrote when you removed a mark. It does not go to the server, and your trainer does not see it.
  • The app has no ads and no usage measurement. When the app crashes, a technical crash report goes to Google (section 7). We do not sell data and do not share it for marketing.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.
  • The app is for ages 13 and up. A trainee aged 13 to 17 joins only with a parent's consent, and the trainer is responsible for getting it. A trainer must be 18 or older.

2. Who we are and how to reach us

FITOPOLIS ("we"). We are responsible for the data kept in the app. For any privacy question, or to use your rights under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law (section 12 below), write to support@fitopolis.co.il.

At this stage the app is in a pilot. Invited trainers and trainees download it on Android, through Google Play's closed testing track.

Your trainer does not work for us. He uses FITOPOLIS to work with you. He is responsible for what he chooses to write about you, and for getting a parent's consent when it is needed (section 13).

3. What is kept, why, and who sees it

How long each thing is kept is in section 10, and how to delete is in section 11.

3.1 The account and signing in

You sign in with an Israeli phone number and a one-time code sent by SMS. The code is valid for 5 minutes, and you can try it 5 times. There is no email and no password, and no sign-in with Google or Apple.

What is kept with the server provider:

  • The phone number you verified.
  • The account name.
  • An internal ID. It is recorded on everything you create in the app, so we know who wrote it.
  • When the account was created, and when you last used it.
  • The IP address of every request to the server. The server provider also uses it to limit how many codes can be requested from each address.

Why: so you can sign in, so a trainee can be linked to the record his trainer opened for him, and to protect the accounts.

On the phone, the sign-in keys are kept in the phone's encrypted storage. When you sign out, they are deleted.

Who sees it: you, us and the server provider. A trainer sees, in the list of who joined with his code, the name or phone number you joined with, and the date.

If you are a trainee, your phone also keeps its time zone on the server, so your day in the app starts and ends at the right hour. The trainer does not see it.

3.2 What the trainer keeps about himself

  • His phone number, name, photo and logo.
  • His settings: notifications and quiet hours, the types of cardio he works with, the list of payment methods he accepts (names only, with no connection to them), colours and logo size, and his meeting settings — when he is available, quotas, holidays and reminders.

What his trainees see of this: his name, photo and logo, the types of cardio, the colours, and the part of the meeting settings meant for them — when a meeting can be requested, how many requests are allowed, and when he is unavailable, with the label he gave it. They also see the times when he is already busy, without names (section 3.8). The trainer's phone number, notification settings, reminders and payment methods never reach them.

3.3 What the trainer writes about the trainee, which only the trainer sees

The trainer opens a record for each client. The trainee does not see these parts of it:

  • The phone number the trainer typed in, and the starting weight.
  • When the coaching started.
  • Private notes and a private reminder. These are free text.
  • The trainee's current phase (bulk, cut, mini-cut or neutral), and text about his nutrition.
  • The invitation code that was sent, and when it expires.
  • If the trainee is 13 to 17: the date the trainer confirmed he received a parent's consent (section 13).
  • Archive dates, which templates were assigned to the client, and whether he already uses the app.
  • How much the trainee pays the trainer and on which plan, and the history of it.
  • The ID of the trainee's account, so the server knows which phone to send a notification to.
  • The notes the trainer writes for himself on a meeting and on a meeting proposal, and the mark that a meeting took place.

Why: so the trainer can run the coaching and track the payments his trainees make to him. FITOPOLIS does not charge money and does not move money.

The trainer may write information about your health in his notes, depending on what he chooses to write. He may also open this record before you joined the app. You can ask to see what is recorded about you (section 12).

3.4 What the trainer and trainee both see

  • Your name, height, age, photo and your colour in the app. Either of you can change the name, height and photo, and the latest change wins. The trainer sets the age and colour.
  • The meal plan and workout program the trainer gave you, and the history of changes to them.
  • When you joined, and when the coaching ended, if it did.
  • The coaching settings: whether meals are marked, whether workouts are logged, how a workout is checked off, wake-up time and bedtime. Either of you can change them, and the trainer sees what you changed. Only you decide whether you mark meals on Saturday, and the trainer sees what you chose.
  • The tracking schedule: when to weigh in, which measurements to take and when, and whether there is a recurring meeting — the day, every how many weeks, the time, the meeting type and the place. The trainer sets it, and you see it.

3.5 The invitation code

The trainer invites a trainee with a link or a QR code. The code works once and is valid for one day. Until the code is used, cancelled or expires, anyone holding it can see, without signing in, the trainer's name and the trainee's first name. He can also join in the trainee's place and see what the trainer has already recorded for him: age, height, meal plan and program. So the code is sent only to the trainee himself.

The link looks like this: fitopolis.co.il/invite/#c=.... The code sits after the # sign, so it never reaches the website's server.

Once a trainee has joined, no other account can join his record with an invitation code. When a trainee changes his phone number, we move the record to the new account, at his request.

3.6 Weigh-ins and measurements

  • Weigh-in: the weight and when you weighed in. You record it, and you both see it. A note you wrote on a weigh-in stays on your phone only.
  • Body measurements: which measurement (by the name the trainer gave it), how many centimetres, and when. The trainer records them, and you both see them.

This is information about your health (section 6).

3.7 Meals, workouts and cardio

  • Marking a meal: which meal, on which day, when you marked it, and when you removed the mark, if you did. Both moments reach the trainer's phone, and a meal you removed the mark from appears to him as not eaten. The reason you wrote when you removed the mark stays on your phone.
  • A workout you did: which workout and its name, when, how long, the volume, and the weight you lifted in each exercise.
  • Cardio: how much, in which unit, and when.

You record them, and the trainer sees them. The note you write for yourself on a workout stays on your phone. This is information about your health (section 6).

3.8 Meetings

  • A meeting: the day, time, length, meeting type, place and topic; if it was cancelled, when and why; whether it did not take place; and whether you confirmed you are coming. The trainer sets the meetings, and you see them. The note the trainer writes for himself on a meeting does not reach you.
  • Meeting requests and proposals: who asked, which time, length and type, topic and place, and the replies — approval, rejection, another proposal or cancellation — with the reason, if you wrote one. Everything you write in a request reaches the trainer, including the reason when you tap "I can't make it". That is what the request is for.
  • A wordless signal: when you act on a request, your phone leaves a short signal on the server with no words in it. It says only that something happened and on which request, so the trainer's phone wakes up.
  • The trainer's busy times: so you do not ask for a time that is already taken, the trainer's phone sends each of his trainees a list of the busy times in the next three months. It holds only the day, time and length, with no name, topic or place. From the list one can tell when the trainer is busy, but not with whom.
  • The phone's calendar: if you choose to add a meeting to your phone's calendar, that stays on the phone only. Your meeting reminder settings stay on the phone too.

3.9 Chat

  • Messages: who sent it and when, the text (up to 4,000 characters), the message you replied to, and whether the message was edited or deleted.
  • Photos and videos: the phone shrinks a photo to screen size before it is sent. A video is compressed on the phone before sending, and is sent only if it is up to 50MB after compression.
  • Voice messages: the recording, its length (up to 10 minutes) and the waveform shown in the bubble. The app records only after you start a recording yourself: you press and hold the microphone button, or lock the recording to go on without a finger. The recording ends when you send or cancel. Nothing else is recorded.
  • A note to the trainer at the end of a workout: if you write it, it is sent to him as a chat message, with the workout's name. The note you write for yourself on a workout is a different thing, and it stays on the phone.
  • Who read what (✓/✓✓): for each side, the app keeps up to which message it has read. The other side sees ✓✓ on his messages you have already read, so he knows roughly when you opened the chat. It works the same way for both sides, and nobody else sees it.
  • Editing and deleting: you can edit and delete only your own message, and only in the first 45 minutes. When a message is deleted, its text and file are deleted, and "message deleted" stays in its place. The trainer cannot delete your message.
  • Where, and who sees it: the chat is only between you and your trainer. Messages are kept on the server, and files are kept in the server provider's file storage. A copy of the files is also kept on the phone of whoever sent them and of whoever received them.

3.10 Templates and payments — the trainer's only

  • Meal-plan templates (name, goal, calorie target, days and swaps), workout templates, and payment templates (monthly amount, date and interval).
  • Payment records: which client, his name at the time of payment, the amount, the date, the payment method, the month and the expected amount.

These are the trainer's records of money his clients pay him. The app does not move money and does not keep credit card or bank account details. The trainee does not see these records. He sees only a confirmation of his own payment (amount and month), if the trainer chose to send one.

3.11 Notifications

  • So the phone can receive notifications, the app registers the phone's notification ID (from Google Firebase) and the phone type with the server provider. When you sign out, the phone cancels the ID at Google and asks the server provider to delete the registration.
  • The notification that passes through Google only wakes up the app. It carries one fixed word and a technical signal, with no name, amount, text or any detail of yours. The notification that appears on your screen is put together by your own phone, from the data it already has.
  • The trainer's phone leaves short wordless notices for the trainee on the server: that his settings were updated, that the meal plan or program changed, that something changed in a meeting, and a payment confirmation (amount and month) if the trainer chose it. The trainee's phone only reads them. The trainer's phone deletes them after 30 days.
