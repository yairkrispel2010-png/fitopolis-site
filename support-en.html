Support

Ran into a problem? A question about FITOPOLIS? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

Two ways to reach us

  • By email. Write to support@fitopolis.co.il and tell us what happened and the phone number you signed up with. A screenshot helps.
  • From inside the app. In "My account", the "Contact the team" row. Write what happened, and the reply comes back there.

We read only what you wrote in the message itself, and nothing else from your account.
